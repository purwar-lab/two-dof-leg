Supported sketch commands: Servo hip;, Servo knee;, attach(), write(), delay(), int variables, assignments, and basic arithmetic (+ - * /). #include and Serial.begin() are ignored. Unsupported commands such as if, for, digitalRead() and custom functions cause an explicit error. No code is executed on your computer or Arduino.
Runs entirely offline in the browser. Playback shows idealized interpolation toward servo targets and may not represent actual servo speed, load, motor torque, collisions, foot-ground contact, or walking stability.
